# 1.1.3 Who Owns "The Internet"?

**Topic:** Ownership of the Internet

**In One Line:** No one owns the Internet. It is a group of many networks that work together using same rules.

**Key Points:**
- The Internet is not owned by a single person, company, or government.
- It is called "Internetwork" or "Internet" - a worldwide collection of networks.
- Networks connect through telephone wires, fiber-optic cables, wireless, and satellite.
- Everything online (social media, games, email, courses) is stored on a server somewhere and connected via the Internet.

**For Interview:**
> The Internet is a worldwide collection of interconnected networks cooperating with each other using common standards.
