# The Bit - Notes

## What is a Bit?

* Computers and networks only work with **binary digits, 0 and 1**.
* All data is stored and transmitted as a series of bits.
* Each bit can have only one of two possible values: **0 or 1**.
* The term **BIT** is an abbreviation of **"binary digit"**.
* Bit is the **smallest piece of data**.
* Humans interpret words and pictures, but computers interpret only **patterns of bits**.

## How is a Bit Stored and Transmitted?

* A bit is stored and transmitted as one of two possible **discrete states**.
* Examples of discrete states:
    - Two directions of magnetization
    - Two distinct voltage or current levels
    - Two distinct levels of light intensity
    - Any other physical system with two discrete states
* Example: A light switch can be either **On or Off**. In binary, these states would correspond to **1 and 0** respectively.

## Input and Output Devices and the Bit

* **Every Input Device** (mouse, keyboard, voice-activated receiver) translates human interaction into **binary code** for the CPU to process and store.
* **Every Output Device** (printer, speakers, monitors, etc.) takes binary data and translates it back into **human recognizable form**.
* Inside the computer itself, **all data is processed and stored as binary**.

## Binary Codes and ASCII

* Computers use binary codes to represent and interpret **letters, numbers and special characters**.
* A commonly used code is **ASCII - American Standard Code for Information Interchange**.
* With ASCII, **each character is represented by eight bits**.

### ASCII Examples:

* Capital letter: **A = 01000001**
* Number: **9 = 00111001**
* Special character: **# = 00100011**

## What is a Byte?

* Each group of **eight bits** is known as a **byte**.
* Example: The representation of a letter or number (like A = 01000001) is one byte.

## Importance of Binary Codes

* Codes can be used to represent almost any type of information digitally including:
    - Computer data
    - Graphics
    - Photos
    - Voice
    - Video
    - Music
