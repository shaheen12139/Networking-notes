# Types of Networks

## 1. Small Home Networks
 Connects a few computers at home to each other and to the Internet.
**Example:** Home WiFi with mobile and laptop.

## 2. Small Office and Home Office (SOHO) Networks
A network for a small business or work-from-home setup.
**Example:** A small shop or office with 10-20 devices, including printers.
**Note:** More secure than home network.

## 3. Medium to Large Networks
Networks used by big companies, schools, or colleges.
**Example:** University or corporate network with 100+ computers.
**Note:** Fast, handles many devices, managed by IT team.

## 4. World Wide Networks (WAN)
#The largest network that connects all small and large networks worldwide.
**Example:** The Internet is the biggest WAN.
**Note:** WAN is made by connecting many LANs.

### Quick Revision Trick:
Small Home < SOHO < Medium/Large < WAN (World Wide)
As name grows, size of network grows.
